Richard and Elizabeth “Betty” Diskin Renew Their Vows After Sixty Years

What therefore God hath joined together, let not man put asunder. Mark 10:9

At St. Columbkille Church in Fort Myers, Florida, Richard and Betty Diskin stood on the altar steps and renewed the vows of sixty years.

A priest in green vestments asked Richard to keep loving her in good times and in bad, in sickness and in health, honoring her all the days of his life. Richard answered yes. Betty was asked to keep the same promise. The priest blessed them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, and said that what God has joined should never be divided.

He told them that after sixty years they already know what to do. They turned toward the church smiling.

That church is their daily life. Richard and Betty attend Mass every day, lead the rosary, and volunteer at St. Columbkille. They are the best parents, grandparents, great-grandparents, and grand-dog parents anyone could ever hope for.

A prayer

Father, bless Richard and Betty. Keep the love You joined sixty years ago faithful to the end. Amen.
